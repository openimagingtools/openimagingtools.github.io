About

Suggest a tool

Open Imaging Tools is a community directory of open-source software for neuroimaging — across all modalities, not just MRI.

MRI has its own site at openmritools.com, maintained by Henrik Formoe (University of Oslo), with 178+ tools and automated weekly health checks. That site is too deep to duplicate here — this hub links out to it.

Every other modality — EEG, MEG, PET, fNIRS, Ultrasound — lives here, community-maintained. If a section is empty, it's waiting for someone to contribute. The scaffold is ready; the community fills it.

What gets included. Tools need to be relevant to the modality, freely available, and actively maintained. New or low-star tools are welcome as long as they solve a real problem and are being worked on.

How to contribute. Use the suggestion form or open an issue directly on GitHub.
