openmritools.com covers MRI — 178+ tools and growing. I had a working template and a domain, so here's the same thing for EEG, PET, MEG, fNIRS, and whatever comes next.

I maintain the site infrastructure and the MRI directory. Everything else is community-driven. If a section is empty, it's waiting for someone to start adding tools. If you want to help, use the suggest form or contribute on GitHub.

What gets included. Tools need to be relevant to the modality, freely available, and actively maintained. New or low-star tools are welcome as long as they solve a real problem and are being worked on.

Started by Henrik Formoe (University of Oslo). Open to everyone.
