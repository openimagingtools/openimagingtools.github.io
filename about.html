About Open Imaging Tools

Suggest a tool

Great tools shouldn't stay hidden — in any modality.

openmritools.com covers MRI. This is the same idea for everything else. Same structure, same health checks, same contribution form. MRI links out to openmritools.com. EEG, MEG, PET, fNIRS, and Ultrasound live here.

Every section is open to contributions. If it's empty, it's waiting for someone to add the first tool. Use the form at /contribute — your submission becomes a public GitHub Issue automatically. You can also open an issue or PR directly on GitHub.

Started by Henrik Formoe (University of Oslo). Open to everyone.
